# Changelog

Every version is published automatically on the [Releases page](https://github.com/FiTH-g1gGLe/Deadzone-Rogue-2-Config-Tool/releases),
and the tool offers it to you the next time you open it.

## v1.0 - First release
- **1.0.0** Finds the game's settings file (demo **Oak** folder, playtest **Valhalla** folder, or any future build) and picks the latest.
  Detects your graphics card, video memory, monitor and refresh rate; keeps the resolution you play at, with native marked and a tip if you're below it.
  Every quality setting written to both the game's menu list and Unreal Engine's scalability values, plus the hidden **Landscape Quality**.
  Native 100% resolution scale, V-Sync and dynamic resolution off, motion blur and film grain off, NVIDIA Reflex + Boost,
  FPS cap at refresh − 3, FOV slider, Fullscreen or Windowed Fullscreen.
  Tick boxes, rich tooltips, backups with one-click restore, read-back check after writing, High CPU priority, auto-updates.
